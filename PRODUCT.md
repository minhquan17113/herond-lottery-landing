# Herond Point — Lottery Ticket Landing

## Register

Brand. A single marketing landing page; design IS the product. No app surfaces in this repo.

## Users & Purpose

Mainstream and crypto-curious browser users arriving from Herond's channels (Discord, Telegram, in-browser promos), mostly on mobile. Job to be done: understand the Season 1 lottery in one scroll and install Herond Browser to start earning daily check-in tickets. Primary conversion: the "Install Herond" CTA.

## Brand personality

Confident, calm, quietly premium. Three words: assured, nocturnal, precise. The page should feel like a well-made ticket in the hand, not a casino.

## Visual identity (committed, preserve)

- Single dark theme (`#101010` base), system font stack by explicit owner preference. Do not add webfonts.
- Brand accent: Herond blue `#3373f6`, supported by purple `#6651ea` and pink `#ff8097` used as atmosphere (WebGL aurora, conic hairlines), never as large flat washes.
- Signature motif: the torn lottery ticket (perforations, notch bites) recurring across pool cards and the final CTA.
- Motion voice: one orchestrated hero intro, restrained scroll reveals, GSAP + Lenis. Reduced motion always honored.

## Anti-references

- AI-vibecode defaults: purple-glow buttons, candy gradients at full saturation, glassmorphism everywhere, eyebrow labels on every section, em-dashes.
- Casino/gambling aesthetics: gold coins, slot machines, flashing jackpot energy. This is a browser loyalty program, not a betting site.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast on the dark theme; `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses all animation; countdown and winner data readable by screen readers.
